=== Mile Assistant - 10-Minute Trial Evaluation ===

This is an evaluation version of Mile Assistant with full feature access for 10 minutes per test session.

--- Evaluation Details ---
* Duration: 10 minutes per test session.
* Live Countdown: The remaining trial time is shown in real-time in the top header [MM:SS left].
* Notifications: A reminder notification will alert you when 1 minute remains.
* Auto-Close: At 10 minutes, a notification prompt will confirm the trial session has concluded and the app will gracefully exit.
* You can restart mile.exe at any time to begin a fresh session.

1. Extract ALL files into a single folder on your PC.

2. Launch mile.exe (Run as Administrator is recommended for global hotkey capture and stealth overlays).
3. If Windows Defender SmartScreen appears ('Windows protected your PC'):
